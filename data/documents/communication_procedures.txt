AVIATION SIMULATION - COMMUNICATION PROCEDURES

Document ID: COMM-001

Purpose:
This document contains simulated communication procedures.

Normal Communication:

When communication is normal, the aircraft can exchange information with the simulated control center.

Degraded Communication:

When communication becomes degraded:

1. Monitor communication status.

2. Attempt to restore the communication channel.

3. Record the communication status in the flight situation.

4. Consult the relevant simulated contingency procedure.

Lost Communication:

When communication is lost:

1. Record the event.

2. Check the available simulated contingency procedures.

3. Continue monitoring the communication system.

Important:
These procedures are simulated and are not intended for real-world aviation operations.
