AVIATION SIMULATION - WEATHER PROCEDURES

Document ID: WEATHER-001

Purpose:
This document contains simulated procedures for handling severe weather conditions in the AeroMind training environment.

Procedure:

1. Monitor the current weather conditions continuously.

2. Identify whether severe weather is located near the planned route.

3. Review available alternative routes.

4. Check the estimated fuel impact of any route modification.

5. Maintain communication with the simulated control center when communication is available.

6. Update the flight situation when weather conditions change.

Important:
These procedures are for simulation and educational purposes only.
