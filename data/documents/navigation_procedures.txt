AVIATION SIMULATION - NAVIGATION PROCEDURES

Document ID: NAV-001

Purpose:
This document contains simulated navigation procedures.

Normal Navigation:

The aircraft uses the simulated navigation system to maintain its planned route.

Degraded Navigation:

When navigation becomes degraded:

1. Identify the navigation status.

2. Check the current simulated position.

3. Review the planned route.

4. Check available navigation alternatives.

5. Record the navigation condition.

Important:
These procedures are part of the AeroMind simulation environment.
They are not real-world flight instructions.
